PureRec Privacy Policy / 隐私政策

Last updated / 更新日期：2026-11-07

1. Scope and provider

PureRec (app.purerec) is provided by KongQBin (GitHub: once512). This policy describes the current version with Google AdMob advertising, Google User Messaging Platform (UMP), and Google Play Billing. Older releases may not contain these features. Recording and local audio processing work without a PureRec account or an audio-upload backend. Advertising, privacy messages and purchase checks use the internet.

2. Recordings and local information

At your request, the microphone captures audio for local recording, playback, waveform analysis, transcoding, trimming and experimental interference reduction. We do not send recordings, filenames, waveforms or integrity manifests to advertising services or a developer server. Local files and settings may include audio parameters, archive associations, selected storage locations, device/input route calibration profiles, quality indicators and integrity manifests containing filenames, timestamps, route details and file hashes. Calibration probe audio is not saved. These functions do not establish a verified identity or legal authenticity of a recording.

3. Advertising and privacy choices

Free use may display native ads in the home and recording lists. Rewarded ads load only when you choose to watch one. Google’s mobile ads SDK collects and shares data for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention: IP address (which can indicate approximate location), app/ad interactions such as launches, taps and video views, diagnostic/performance information, and device or account identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID where available. Google states that this SDK traffic uses TLS in transit. We do not add a separate analytics SDK or send audio content as ad targeting data. UMP handles the applicable privacy choices and related consent records. Use Advertising and privacy in Settings to review available choices; availability depends on region and Google configuration. Non-personalized or limited ads can still involve data processing. Android settings provide advertising ID controls. Active Pro access suppresses new ad requests and removes ad placements, but an already initialized SDK may still communicate internally; Pro does not mean that the APK contains no advertising SDK.

4. Purchases and temporary access

Google Play processes payments and subscription transactions. The app receives purchase information, including product identifiers, purchase tokens and signed purchase/state information, to verify and acknowledge purchases with Google Play. Local preferences store entitlement status, verification times and rewarded-access expiry/event identifiers. We do not operate a purchase-verification server or receive card numbers through the app. Google and its payment providers handle payment details; Google Play may make order and transaction records available to the developer for support and order management. No separate PureRec account is created. Reward balances are local and are not synchronized between devices.

5. Permissions and device access

Microphone permission enables user-requested recording and calibration. Foreground-service permissions and notifications support recording, playback and long-running local processing/import/export. Wake locks help ongoing work continue; vibration can signal recording warnings. Internet/network-state permissions support Google services. Advertising ID and other SDK permissions may be included by Google dependencies; they do not grant microphone access to advertisements. Folder access is granted through Android’s system picker, not unrestricted storage access. You can revoke microphone, notification and folder permissions in Android, with corresponding features affected.

6. Storage, sharing, retention and deletion

Recordings are retained until you delete them or remove their storage. Private app files, settings and local rewards are normally removed by clearing app data or uninstalling; export important content first. Files saved to a selected external location, exported copies and recipients’ copies can remain and must be managed separately. Pending working files may be retained for recovery after interrupted saving. Integrity JSON stays private unless you explicitly export it, including when exporting a recording group. Sharing or choosing an external document provider sends content to that chosen destination; a cloud provider may upload it under its own policy. PureRec disables Android automatic app backup in its configuration, but cannot control copies made by users, device vendors or external services. Deletion is not a promise of secure erasure. Google retains its own advertising/payment data under its policies; uninstalling PureRec does not delete those records or cancel subscriptions.

7. Support, rights and international processing

If you email us, we receive the address, message and attachments you choose to send and use them to respond and resolve the issue. Send only necessary information; recordings are not required for ordinary support. Support correspondence is retained as needed to handle the request and applicable legal obligations; you may request deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, obtain portability or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to exercise applicable rights; we cannot retrieve recordings held only on your device or directly erase Google’s records. Google and email/storage providers may process data outside your country under their policies and applicable safeguards. We do not set a retention period on behalf of Google. Children and guardians should contact us if they believe a child’s information has been provided improperly; any legally required parental consent must be obtained before use.

8. Updates and contact

We update this policy when data practices change and provide notices or request consent where required. Contact the provider at kong5664246@gmail.com. Related information: Google Privacy Policy, Google mobile ads data disclosure, and our Terms of Service.

1. 适用范围与提供者

PureRec（app.purerec）由 KongQBin（GitHub：once512）提供。本政策描述包含 Google AdMob 广告、Google 用户消息平台（UMP）及 Google Play Billing 的当前版本；旧版本可能不包含这些功能。录音及本地音频处理无需注册 PureRec 账号，也不使用录音上传后端。广告、隐私消息及购买核验需要联网。

2. 录音与本地信息

应用仅在您发起操作时使用麦克风，录音、播放、波形分析、转码、裁切及实验性抗干扰处理在本机完成。我们不会将录音、文件名、波形或完整性清单发送给广告服务或开发者服务器。本地文件和设置可能包含音频参数、归档关联、所选存储位置、设备及输入路由校准画像、质量指标，以及包含文件名、时间、路由信息和文件哈希的完整性清单。校准探测音频不保存。这些功能不构成身份核验或录音法律真实性认证。

3. 广告与隐私选择

免费使用时，首页及录音列表可能显示原生广告；激励广告仅在您主动选择观看后加载。Google 移动广告 SDK 会为广告、分析及防欺诈收集和共享数据，包括 IP 地址（可推断大致位置）、应用及广告互动（如启动、点击、视频观看）、诊断及性能信息，以及可用的广告 ID、应用集 ID 等设备或账号标识。Google 说明该 SDK 的网络传输使用 TLS。我们没有额外接入独立分析 SDK，也不将音频内容用于广告定向。UMP 处理适用的隐私选择及相关同意记录。您可以在设置的“广告与隐私”入口查看可用选项，具体取决于地区及 Google 配置。非个性化或受限广告仍可能涉及数据处理；Android 设置提供广告 ID 管理。有效 Pro 权益会停止新的广告请求并移除广告位，但已初始化的 SDK 可能仍进行内部通信；Pro 不代表安装包不含广告 SDK。

4. 购买与临时权益

Google Play 处理付款和订阅交易。应用接收商品标识、购买令牌及已签名的购买和状态信息，用于核验并向 Google Play 确认购买。本地偏好保存权益状态、核验时间及激励权益的到期时间和事件标识。我们没有购买核验服务器，也不通过应用接收银行卡号。支付资料由 Google 及其支付服务商处理；Google Play 可能向开发者提供用于订单管理和支持的订单及交易记录。购买不会创建独立的 PureRec 账号。广告奖励余额仅在本地保存，不跨设备同步。

5. 权限与设备访问

麦克风权限用于您发起的录音及校准。前台服务权限和通知用于录音、播放及耗时的本地处理、导入、导出；唤醒锁辅助任务持续运行，振动用于录音告警。网络及网络状态权限用于 Google 服务。Google 依赖可能合并广告 ID 等 SDK 权限，这不意味着广告获得麦克风访问权。文件夹访问通过 Android 系统选择器授权，不申请全盘管理权限。您可以在 Android 中撤回麦克风、通知及目录权限，相应功能可能受影响。

6. 存储、分享、保留与删除

录音保留至您删除或移除其存储。清除应用数据或卸载通常会移除私有文件、设置和本地奖励，请先导出重要内容。保存到所选外部位置的文件、导出副本及接收者副本可能继续保留，需要单独管理。保存中断后的工作文件可能保留以供恢复。完整性 JSON 默认仅保存在私有目录，只有您主动导出（包括导出录音组）时才复制出去。分享或选择外部文档提供者会将内容交给您选择的目标；云端提供者可能依其政策上传内容。PureRec 在配置中关闭 Android 自动应用备份，但无法控制用户、设备厂商或外部服务制作的副本。删除不保证安全擦除。Google 依据其政策保留广告及支付数据；卸载 PureRec 不会删除这些记录或取消订阅。
