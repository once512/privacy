Privacy Policy for PureRec

Last updated: October 2026

PureRec ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection and usage when you use the PureRec mobile application.

1. Information Collection and Use

Audio Recording: PureRec requires access to your device's microphone (RECORD_AUDIO permission) solely to capture voice recordings upon your request. All recorded audio files are processed and stored locally on your device storage. We do not upload, listen to, or share your voice recordings on any remote servers.

Personal Data: PureRec does not collect, log, or sell any personally identifiable information (such as your name, email address, or phone number) during the normal use of recording features.

2. In-App Purchases and Subscriptions

All financial transactions and subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play. We do not collect or store your payment details, credit card numbers, or billing addresses.

3. Permissions Used

  • Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO): Required to capture high-quality audio recordings.
  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS): Used to show a foreground service notification while recording is in progress.

4. Third-Party Services

The app may use Google Play Services for in-app billing. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy regarding how transaction data is handled.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted directly on this page.
