Privacy Policy

This policy describes ZipFox for Android, application ID app.zipfox, version 0.1.5 (6), provided by KongQBin (GitHub: once512, “we” or “us”). Contact: kong5664246@gmail.com.

1. What ZipFox does

ZipFox creates and extracts archives on your device. It does not require a ZipFox account. Our file-processing code does not upload selected files, archive contents, filenames, paths or passwords to us or to advertising services. We do not operate a file-storage or purchase-verification backend for this version. Google advertising, privacy-choice and purchase services can communicate over the network; local file processing does not mean the entire app is network-free.

This version includes Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0, User Messaging Platform (UMP) 4.0.0 and Google Play Billing 9.1.0. Debug builds use official test ads. Release builds support separately configured native ad placements for compression and extraction, rewarded ads, and Google Play products. Actual ads and purchases depend on platform configuration and availability; an unavailable purchase does not grant access or simulate payment.

2. Storage and other permissions

  • Files: Android 11 and later use “All files access” (MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE) to browse shared storage, select files and folders, read archives and volumes, create outputs and perform deletion or cleanup you request. This grants broad storage access, beyond a single selected file, subject to Android restrictions. Android 8–10 use read/write storage permissions. You can revoke access in Android settings; file operations requiring it will then be unavailable.
  • Foreground service and notifications: These support an ongoing archive task, progress, cancellation and completion/error notices. Notifications may include an output name or an error containing a path; visibility on a lock screen depends on your device settings. Notification permission can be managed separately in Android settings.
  • Network and Google services: Internet/network-state access supports ads, privacy choices and purchases. The merged SDK manifest also includes Billing, advertising-ID, Android advertising-service and wake-lock permissions. ZipFox does not request microphone, camera, contacts or runtime location permissions. IP-based approximate location does not require a location permission.

The current file browser accesses device storage directly; cloud document providers through Android's Storage Access Framework are not integrated. A destination independently backed up or synchronized by another app or device service remains subject to that service's settings and privacy policy.

3. Information handled on your device

  • Files and metadata: Processing reads selected content, filenames, directory structure, sizes and timestamps and writes archives or extracted files to your chosen location. Archives can contain filenames and other metadata. Password-protected ZIP does not hide entry names; password-protected 7Z also encrypts archive headers. Output names remain visible in storage.
  • Passwords: Passwords are used in memory for the requested operation, are not deliberately saved to preferences, activity saved state or service Intents, and are not sent to us. Task character buffers are cleared when processing ends, but this is not a guarantee that every runtime or library copy is immediately erased. We have no password recovery copy.
  • Task and interface state: File paths, output location/name, format and compression level may be kept in Android activity state to restore the screen. In-memory progress and results support the current task. This version does not provide automatic task recovery after process termination.
  • Local preferences and access records: The app saves separate source-deletion and failure-cleanup settings for compression/extraction, the extraction top-level-folder setting, dismissed warnings, the local allowance date, successful task counts, unused reward credits and their weekly reset boundary, reward identifiers used to avoid duplicate credit, and the last Pro-verification time and cached validity deadline. These support settings, quotas and purchase access; the app does not send its local quota records to a developer server.

ZipFox disables Android automatic app backup and excludes its app data from the configured cloud-backup and device-transfer rules. This does not prevent independent backup or synchronization of files in shared storage.

4. Temporary files, deletion and retention

Processing creates .zipfox-* working directories in the output location. They can contain plaintext extracted files, intermediate archives and backups of files being replaced. They are not a secure vault. Successful operations attempt cleanup. “Clean process files on failure” defaults to on; turning it off preserves working files after failure or cancellation. A crash, force-stop, power loss or unsuccessful rollback may leave files behind. When rollback fails, recovery backups may be retained even if cleanup is enabled; check the reported path before deleting anything.

“Delete source files after success” defaults to off and is saved separately for each mode. If enabled, the app checks the completed output and source snapshot before attempting ordinary source deletion; changed or inaccessible sources may remain. Extraction can replace same-name regular files in the destination. Review the result and warnings and keep independent backups.

Source files, outputs and retained working files remain in their storage location until you or another authorized process removes them. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes private preferences and local access records, but does not automatically remove files or working directories in shared storage. Revoke storage permissions through Android settings and manage remaining files with a file manager. Ordinary deletion does not guarantee secure erasure from flash storage, backups or synchronized copies.

5. Advertising and privacy choices

Non-Pro users may see native video ads and may choose a rewarded ad for one additional compression or extraction opportunity. Google Mobile Ads collects and shares IP addresses, device/account identifiers, product interactions and diagnostic information for ads, analytics and fraud prevention. IP addresses can indicate approximate location. Google states SDK data in transit is protected with TLS. This SDK disclosure is separate from ZipFox's local file processing.

Production ad initialization is gated by UMP's ad-request decision after updating consent information and showing a form where required. An “Advertising privacy settings” entry appears in the Pro dialog when UMP requires it. You can also use Android advertising-ID controls and Google's ad controls. Available choices and personalization depend on region, settings and consent. Declining an optional ad choice does not itself remove your free daily file-processing allowance. Active Pro hides ad placements and stops new native/rewarded ad loading; billing and privacy-service communication may still occur.

See the Google Privacy Policy and Google Mobile Ads data disclosure for Google's handling and account controls.

6. Purchases and local access records

Where configured and offered, monthly Pro renews automatically and permanent Pro is a one-time purchase for ZipFox. Google Play handles payment and account information. The app receives product details, purchase tokens, signed purchase records and transaction/acknowledgment states to validate, acknowledge and restore access. We do not receive your card number or billing address through the app. The current implementation verifies purchase signatures on the device without a developer-operated verification backend.

The app stores a verified Pro flag as timestamps for up to 24 hours of offline use and queries ownership again through Play. Pending payments do not activate access. Free access permits three successful compression tasks and three successful extraction tasks per device-local calendar day; failed or cancelled tasks do not consume a use. Daily free uses are consumed before rewarded credits. Credits remain usable across days within the same week and expire at Monday 00:00 according to the device local calendar; expiry is applied when the app next checks the balance. Turning the clock back does not restore expired credits. Daily counts and credits are device-local; clearing data or uninstalling can remove them. Valid paid purchases can be restored through the same Google Play account, subject to successful verification.

Manage renewal in Google Play subscriptions. Uninstalling or buying permanent Pro does not cancel an existing monthly subscription. Google-held purchase and advertising records follow Google's retention rules and account controls; clearing ZipFox data does not delete them.

7. Support, sharing and international processing

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request and investigate the issue, and retain them only as needed for that purpose or applicable legal obligations. Avoid sending passwords or confidential files. You can request deletion of support correspondence; any legally required retention will be explained.

We do not sell your selected files or provide them to advertisers. Google processes SDK and payment data under its own policies and may process it in countries outside yours. Support email is handled by the email provider under its policy. We may disclose support information where legally required or necessary to protect legal rights, subject to applicable law. These statements do not replace any separate consent or safeguards required for a particular transfer.

8. Your choices, children and updates

You can manage permissions, remove local files, clear app data, manage Google privacy settings and contact us about access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent where applicable. We cannot access or erase files held only on your device remotely. We will explain any limits on a request; you may also contact your competent data-protection authority where available.

ZipFox is a general file utility and is not specifically directed to children. We do not ask for age or operate child accounts. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

We will update this policy when practices change and revise the date above. Material changes will be communicated through an appropriate channel, with any notice or consent required by law. Questions and privacy requests: kong5664246@gmail.com.

1. 应用功能与数据处理概况

ZipFox 在您的设备上创建和解压归档文件，无需注册 ZipFox 账户。我们的文件处理代码不会向我们或广告服务上传您选择的文件、压缩包内容、文件名、路径或密码。当前版本没有开发者运营的文件存储或购买验证后端。Google 广告、隐私选项和购买服务可能联网，本地处理文件不意味着整个应用不联网。

本版本接入 Google Mobile Ads 25.5.0、User Messaging Platform（UMP）4.0.0 和 Google Play Billing 9.1.0。Debug 版本使用官方测试广告。发布版支持分别配置压缩页与解压页原生广告、激励广告及 Google Play 商品。实际广告与购买取决于平台配置和可用性；不可用的购买不会授予权益或模拟支付。

2. 存储及其他权限

  • 文件访问：Android 11 及以上通过“所有文件访问权限”（MANAGE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE）浏览共享存储、选择文件与文件夹、读取归档及分卷、创建输出，并执行您要求的删除或清理。这是超出单个所选文件的广泛存储权限，仍受 Android 系统限制。Android 8–10 使用存储读写权限。您可在系统设置撤回授权，相关文件操作将无法继续使用。
  • 前台服务与通知：用于持续执行归档任务、显示进度、取消任务以及完成或错误通知。通知可能包含输出名称或带路径的错误信息；锁屏可见性取决于设备设置。通知权限可在系统设置中单独管理。
  • 网络与 Google 服务：互联网和网络状态权限用于广告、隐私选项及购买。SDK 合并清单还包含支付、广告标识符、Android 广告服务及唤醒锁权限。ZipFox 不申请麦克风、相机、通讯录或运行时位置权限。通过 IP 推断大致位置不需要位置权限。

当前文件浏览器直接访问设备存储，尚未接入 Android 存储访问框架中的云端文档提供方。其他应用或设备服务自行备份、同步的存储位置，仍适用相应服务的设置与隐私政策。

3. 在设备上处理的信息

  • 文件和元数据：处理时读取所选内容、文件名、目录结构、大小及时间信息，并向您指定的位置写入压缩包或解压文件。归档可能包含文件名等元数据。加密 ZIP 不隐藏包内条目名称；加密 7Z 同时加密归档头。输出文件名在存储中仍可见。
  • 密码：密码在内存中用于当前操作，不主动保存到偏好设置、Activity 保存状态或服务 Intent，也不发送给我们。任务结束时清空任务字符缓冲区，但不保证运行时或第三方库的每个副本均立即擦除。我们没有密码恢复副本。
  • 任务与界面状态：文件路径、输出位置与名称、格式及压缩等级可能保存在 Android Activity 状态中，用于恢复界面；内存中的进度和结果用于当前任务。当前版本不会在进程终止后自动恢复任务。
  • 本地设置与权益记录：保存压缩与解压各自的源文件删除、失败清理选项，解压顶层目录选项，已关闭的提示，本地额度日期、成功任务次数、未使用的奖励次数及每周重置边界、防止重复发奖的奖励标识，以及 Pro 最近验证时间和缓存有效截止时间。这些信息用于设置、额度和购买权益；应用不会将本地额度记录发送到开发者服务器。

ZipFox 关闭 Android 应用自动备份，并通过当前配置的云备份和设备迁移规则排除应用数据。这不阻止共享存储中文件被其他服务独立备份或同步。

4. 过程文件、删除与保存期限

处理会在输出位置创建 .zipfox-* 过程目录，其中可能包含明文解压内容、中间归档及被替换文件的备份。这些目录不是安全保险箱。成功后尝试清理；“失败时清理过程文件”默认开启，关闭后失败或取消会保留过程文件。崩溃、强制停止、断电或回滚失败可能留下文件。回滚失败时，即使开启清理也可能保留恢复备份；删除前请核对提示的路径。

“成功后删除源文件”默认关闭，压缩和解压分别保存选择。开启后，应用检查完成的输出和源文件快照，再尝试普通删除；发生变化或无法访问的源文件可能保留。解压可能覆盖输出位置的同名普通文件。请查看结果与警告并保留独立备份。

源文件、输出及保留的过程文件留在相应位置，直到您或其他有权进程删除。清除应用数据或卸载会移除私有设置及本地权益记录，但不会自动删除共享存储中的文件和过程目录。您可通过系统设置撤回存储授权，通过文件管理器处理剩余文件。普通删除不保证擦除闪存、备份或同步副本中的所有数据。

5. 广告与隐私选项

非 Pro 用户可能看到原生视频广告，也可自愿观看激励广告获取一次额外压缩或解压机会。Google Mobile Ads 为广告、分析及反欺诈收集和共享 IP 地址、设备或账户标识符、产品交互及诊断信息；IP 地址可反映大致位置。Google 说明 SDK 传输数据使用 TLS 保护。这些 SDK 数据处理与 ZipFox 的本地文件处理分别进行。

正式广告初始化会先更新 UMP 同意信息、按需展示表单，并以 UMP 是否允许请求广告的结果为准。UMP 要求时，Pro 弹窗会显示“广告隐私设置”入口。您也可以使用 Android 广告标识符设置及 Google 广告设置。可用选项及广告个性化取决于地区、设置与同意状态。拒绝可选广告同意本身不会取消每日免费文件处理额度。Pro 有效时隐藏广告位并停止新的原生／激励广告加载，但支付与隐私服务仍可能联网。

Google 的处理方式与账户控制详见 Google 隐私政策及 Google Mobile Ads 数据披露。

请在 Google Play 订阅设置中管理续订。卸载或购买永久 Pro 不会取消现有月订阅。Google 持有的购买与广告记录适用其保存规则及账户控制，清除 ZipFox 数据不会删除这些记录。
